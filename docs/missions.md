# GTA 6 Mission List: Every Story Mission in Order

**How many missions are in GTA 6?** Most guides count **91 main story missions** across **5 chapters**. That's more than GTA 5's 69, and close to Red Dead Redemption 2's 104. TalkEsport counts **93** using the in-game Story recap tab, saying early lists missed two Chapter 2 missions. We'll update this page when that's settled.

| Chapter | Title | Missions |
| :--- | :--- | ---: |
| 1 | Just Like Paradise | 22 |
| 2 | Paradise Lost | 11 *(13 per TalkEsport)* |
| 3 | Tits, Ass, Drug Money | 26 |
| 4 | Vice City, Baby | 25 |
| 5 | Top of the World, Ma | 7 |

Spoiler-heavy mission names are hidden behind a click.
