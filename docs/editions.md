# GTA 6 Price, Editions & Download Size

## How much is GTA 6?

| Edition | US price | Platforms |
| :--- | ---: | :--- |
| **Standard Edition** | $79.99 | PS5, Xbox Series X\|S |
| **Ultimate Edition** | $99.99 | PS5, Xbox Series X\|S |
| Ultimate Edition Upgrade (Xbox Store) | $20 | Xbox Series X\|S |

The physical version is **Standard Edition only**, and it's a **code in a box, not a disc**. Prices vary by region and can change, so check the store before buying. Sources: [Just Jared](https://www.justjared.com/2026/11/17/gta-6-price-editions-standard-ultimate-preorder-bonuses/), [Technobezz](https://www.technobezz.com/gta-6-editions).

## GTA 6 download size

- **Up to 212 GB** of free space, per Rockstar and PlayStation's own guidance.
- PS5 may also need a **system software update** (~1.3 GB, player-reported).

## Release time

GTA 6 unlocked at **midnight local time on November 19, 2026**. US storefronts used Eastern time, so western time zones could play on the evening of November 18 ([Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/brianmazique/2026/11/12/gta-6-unlocks-at-midnight-on-november-19-but-millions-of-americans-can-start-playing-the-night-before/)).
